4. How to Think About Likelihood Ratios

Likelihood Ratio Plain-English Meaning
0.1Strong evidence against the hypothesis
0.5Moderate evidence against the hypothesis
1.0No change
2.0Moderate evidence for the hypothesis
5.0Strong evidence for the hypothesis
10.0Very strong evidence for the hypothesis

Calculation method: probability is converted to odds, multiplied by the likelihood ratio, then converted back to probability. This makes it easy to update repeatedly as additional evidence arrives.
